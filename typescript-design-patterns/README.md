# typescript-design-patterns

Select a small design from the actual source of variation and its ownership requirements. The [skill workflow](SKILL.md) covers the source's 22 patterns while checking behavioral contracts, errors, state, and resource lifetime.

## Use

`Use $typescript-design-patterns to choose a design for interchangeable payment providers and verify its lifecycle contract.`

Copy this complete folder, including references and `LICENSE.upstream`, into the skill location supported by your agent.

## References

- [Creational patterns](references/creational.md): factories, builders, cloning, and shared initialization.
- [Structural patterns](references/structural.md): adapters, facades, wrappers, proxies, composites, and shared data.
- [Behavioral patterns](references/behavioral.md): strategies, states, events, commands, undo, chains, iterators, and visitors.
